Waywaker privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Waywaker is an Android app that wakes you before you reach your stop. This page explains what it does with your information.

In short

Waywaker has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you put in the app stays on your phone. We (the developer) never receive your location, your trips or anything else about you.

Location

During a trip Waywaker uses your phone's location, including when the app is closed or the screen is off, to know how close you are to your stop and ring the alarm in time. A notification shows while this happens. Location is used only on the phone, is never sent to us or anyone else, and tracking stops when the trip ends.

What is stored on your phone

Your name, avatar, colour and optional photo; saved places; alarm settings and any song you choose; and your trip history (where you went, when, and whether the alarm woke you). It is kept in the app's private storage, is not included in phone backups, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can remove places, trips and the photo in the app at any time.

Internet use

Waywaker goes online for two things only:

  • Map images, from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org).
  • Offline place files you choose to download, from GitHub (github.com).

Like any website, these services see your phone's IP address and what is downloaded, which for the map means the area you are looking at. No name, account or trip details are sent. Their own privacy policies apply: openfreemap.org and GitHub's site policy.

Camera, photos, files and voice

A profile photo and an alarm song are picked with Android's own camera, photo picker and file picker, and copied into the app. Voice search opens your phone's voice input; Waywaker only receives the words it recognises, and does not record audio.

Permissions

Location (trip tracking), notifications and full-screen alarm (to wake you), exact alarms (to warn you if the app was stopped), and running in the background without battery limits (so the alarm is not delayed). You can turn any of these off in Android settings; the alarm may then be late or not ring.

Children

Waywaker is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them. It collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and in the app, with a new date.
